42. 2026-10-17 Observation only, no code change: how the agreement score is built (read from calculate_consensus_reliability, read-only). The function selects every row of ai_consensus_audit with no filter on strategy_status. A record is skipped when either the Groq or the Gemini answer does not parse as valid; otherwise it counts. Base score = 50% ASSESSMENT-word match (for example BEARISH vs BEARISH) + 30% SIMULATED_OUTCOME match + 20% share of rows whose consensus_status is exactly AGREEMENT. Trend = (mean quality of the last 4 valid records minus the mean of all older ones) x 0.20, capped at +-10, with AGREEMENT 100, PARTIAL_AGREEMENT 50, CONFLICT or other 0.
